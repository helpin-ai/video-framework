Copy structure borrowed from Ferndesk's landing page ("Sound familiar? This month you changed a default…
your docs were last updated 3 months ago" → "Stop hoping… Know." → "Nothing publishes without you"), rewritten
in Helpin's own words and backed by our site. Concept: your week is a timeline; the camera whips along it,
rewinds 12 weeks to your docs, and the fixed guide rides it forward to this week.

| Bars | Screen | On screen |
|---|---|---|
| 0–1 | MON | "You renamed a button." The button label is selected and retyped: Start sync → Continue |
| 1–2 | WED | "Moved a setting." Integrations lifts and jumps to the top of Settings |
| 2–3 | FRI | "Added a step." The flow parts, Confirm workspace drops in; v2.6 SHIPPED flag |
| 3–4.5 | rewind | Camera rewinds 12 weeks: "And your docs?" The guide pops up; stamp LAST UPDATED 3 MONTHS AGO |
| 4.5–5.5 | type | "Sound familiar?" → "Stop guessing." |
| 5.5 | drop | "Quill checks." (Quill avatar), shrinks into a header whose verb rolls |
| 6–8.5 | checks | Live Quill preview (helpin.ai/products/knowledge): request → review steps → proposal → ready for review |
| 8.5–10.5 | captures | Quill clicks Connect in the product, the new step appears, shutter, refined capture |
| 10.5–12.5 | fixes | Stamp peels, old step struck, new step typed, new screenshot drops in |
| 12.5–14 | you publish | "Nothing goes live without you." Your cursor clicks Publish → Published ✓ |
| 14–15.5 | payoff | The guide rides the timeline from 12 WEEKS AGO to FRI · THIS WEEK; stamp UPDATED THIS WEEK; "Your docs. Caught up." |
| 15.5–17.5 | tag | "You ship. / Your docs keep up." Helpin mark, helpin.ai, Open source · Coming soon |

Music: UK garage 2-step (its own): shuffled percussion hook to the drop, then a full 2-step groove, no end hit.
